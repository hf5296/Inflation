ℹ️ Understanding the Comparison

Logarithmic Scale

Charts use a log scale so assets with vastly different prices (like $88k BTC vs $4k Gold) can be compared visually. Equal vertical distances = equal percentage changes.

Common Date Range

Data automatically starts from when ALL selected assets have data. For BTC + Gold, this means data starts from 2009 (when BTC was created).

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)

The annual rate of return that would be required for an investment to grow from its beginning value to its ending value. Higher CAGR = better long-term performance.

Actual USD Prices

All values shown are real historical prices in USD, not normalized index values. This gives you a true picture of how each asset has performed.

💡 Key Insights

  • ✓ Since 1913, the US Dollar has lost over 96% of its purchasing power due to inflation
  • ✓ Gold has risen from $20.67/oz in 1913 to over $2,300/oz today, largely tracking inflation
  • ✓ Bitcoin has been the best performing asset of the decade, rising from $0.001 in 2009 to $88,000+ today
  • ✓ Assets that grow faster than CPI preserve purchasing power over time
