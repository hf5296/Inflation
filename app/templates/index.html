🛡️ Inflation Hedges: Assets That Fight Back

While the dollar loses value, some assets tend to go UP in price. People call these "inflation hedges" because they can help protect your savings. Below are three popular ones — notice how their prices have grown while the dollar shrank.

₿ Bitcoin — Digital Gold

Created in 2009, Bitcoin is like digital gold — there will only ever be 21 million coins, and no government can print more. It's the best-performing asset in history, though it's very volatile (big price swings).

ɱ Monero — Private Digital Cash

Monero is like Bitcoin, but with privacy built-in. Your transactions are hidden from view, like physical cash. It's favored by those who value financial privacy.

🥇 Gold — The Original Store of Value

Gold has been valued for thousands of years. It can't be printed, doesn't rust, and has kept its value through wars, recessions, and the rise and fall of empires.

📈 From $20/oz in 1913 to over $2,600/oz today — Gold has roughly kept pace with inflation over 100+ years.
